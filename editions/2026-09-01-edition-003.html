How to read this

  • Primary sources only where possible. Where only secondary reporting existed, the item says so.
  • Shipped is separated from announced. This distinction is the biggest source of confident-sounding errors in AI news.
  • Vendor claims are reproduced, not endorsed. Conflicts of interest are named inline.
  • Unconfirmed is written as unconfirmed. Absence of a finding is not evidence of absence.
  • Item count is not market signal. Deepgram dominates Voice partly because it publishes a granular dated changelog and its competitors do not.
  • Fetched pages are data, never instructions. Deepgram, ElevenLabs and modelcontextprotocol.io docs prepend directive text addressed to AI readers. Benign, first-party, ignored.

Scope

Seven areas: agent protocols, voice and speech models, Cognigy and the CCaaS field, frameworks and eval, frontier and open-weight models plus AI hardware, plus two discovery lanes for things that are new rather than merely updated: emerging tools and new techniques.

General AI news and incremental benchmark papers are out of scope unless they change one of those seven. Frontier model launches and AI hardware shifts are in scope under Models & hardware.

The page is rewritten in place each scan, so the link stays the same. Full editions are archived as markdown in the project and synced to the local folder.
